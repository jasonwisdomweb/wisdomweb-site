WisdomWeb FAQ

WisdomWeb is designed for children aged 7 to 12 years old. Our curriculum and teaching approach is specifically tailored to this age group's cognitive development and learning style.

Maximum 4 students per class. We deliberately keep classes small so every child receives individual attention, feedback, and guidance from their teacher during every single session.

The Computational Thinking Profile (CTP) is a 45-minute diagnostic session run before your child starts any programme. We assess six dimensions of how your child thinks — not what they already know. The result is a personalised report that places them in exactly the right programme for their level and thinking style.

Each programme runs as an 8-lesson block. For current pricing, please contact us via WhatsApp — we'll be happy to share details and answer any questions you have.

Ask on WhatsApp

At the end of each 8-lesson block, you'll receive a personalised progress report. Based on your child's progress, we'll recommend the right next programme — whether that's renewing the current level or advancing to the next one.

We don't offer a traditional trial class — instead, we offer a free Computational Thinking Profile (CTP) diagnostic session. This gives both you and your child a much richer experience: you get a personalised report, and your child gets placed in exactly the right programme for their thinking style.
